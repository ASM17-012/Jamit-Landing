Chapter 1General Provisions

Article 1Purpose #

These Terms set out the rights, obligations, and responsibilities of the Operator and members, the conditions and procedures for using the Service, and other necessary matters in connection with the use of the mobile application service “JAMIT” (the “Service”) provided by Minjae Kim (김민재) (the “Operator”; contact: support@jamit.kr).

Article 2Definitions #

The terms used in these Terms have the following meanings.

  1. “Service” means the JAMIT mobile application and all related services provided by the Operator that allow members to complete a jam by uploading performance videos for each part (instrument, vocals, etc.) based on a specific section of an original song posted on YouTube, and to create, save, and share an Its that combines the participating tracks.
  2. “Member” means a person who has agreed to these Terms, entered into a service agreement through social login, and uses the Service.
  3. “Original song” means music video content posted on YouTube that serves as the basis of a jam. Original songs are not stored or distributed by the Service; they are played through YouTube.
  4. “Post” (or “Jam”) means a posting in which a member specifies a section of an original song and the parts to be recruited in order to recruit participants for a jam.
  5. “Track” means a performance video for a part (including video and audio) that a member records and uploads to join a post.
  6. “Its” means a short video created by combining the tracks gathered in a post and compositing them in a grid layout or similar format; its creator may save it to their device or share it outside the Service.
  7. “Chat” means the feature through which a member exchanges messages with other members within the Service, and includes both conversations between two members and chat rooms in which multiple members participate (including chat rooms attached to a Space).
  8. “Message” means text that a member sends in a chat, and posts, tracks, and Its that a member shares by including them in a message.
  9. “Content” means any and all materials that a member posts or uploads to the Service, including posts, tracks, Its, profiles, text, and messages.

Article 3Posting and Amendment of the Terms #

  1. The Operator posts these Terms on screens within the Service and on information pages so that members can easily review them.
  2. The Operator may amend these Terms to the extent that doing so does not violate relevant laws, such as the Act on the Regulation of Terms and Conditions and the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection.
  3. When amending the Terms, the Operator will give notice, stating the effective date and the reasons for the amendment, by in-Service notice, push notification, or other means, beginning 7 days before the effective date (or 30 days before, for changes that are unfavorable to members or material).
  4. If a member continues to use the Service on or after the effective date of the amended Terms, the member is deemed to have agreed to the amended Terms. A member who does not agree to the amended Terms may delete their account in accordance with Article 7.

Article 4Rules Not Covered by the Terms #

Matters not provided for in these Terms, and the interpretation of these Terms, are governed by relevant laws and general commercial practice. Matters concerning the processing of personal information are governed by the separate JAMIT Privacy Policy.

  1. An account may be used only by the member themselves and may not be transferred or lent to anyone else.

The Operator is not liable for any disadvantage arising from a member’s failure to manage these properly unless the Operator acted intentionally or negligently.
  3. If a member’s information, such as their profile, changes, the member must update it within the Service.

Article 7Account Deletion #

  1. Members may terminate the service agreement (delete their account) at any time using the in-app account deletion feature.
  2. Upon account deletion, the Operator invalidates the member’s login sessions and push tokens and also handles the removal of the social login link (Kakao, Google, Apple). For an Apple account, account deletion is processed only once the unlink has been completed; for Kakao and Google accounts, the link information held by the Operator is destroyed even if the unlink request fails or unlinking is not supported in the app environment. The member may remove the link directly in the account settings of each platform, and the details of this processing are governed by Article 7 of the Privacy Policy.
  3. The personal information of a member who has deleted their account is destroyed as set out in the JAMIT Privacy Policy. If you delete your account, the messages you sent will no longer be visible on the other party’s screen, and if no one remains in a chat room with multiple participants, the messages in that room, including those written by other members, follow the same procedure. Retention periods are governed by Article 3 of the Privacy Policy.
  4. Content uploaded by a member who has deleted their account (tracks, posts, Its) is handled as follows.
    1. Tracks uploaded by the member (originals, processed versions, and thumbnails) are no longer visible in the Service upon account deletion and are destroyed after the period set out in Article 3 of the Privacy Policy has passed.
    2. Its created by the member are handled in the same manner, whether or not they have been published. Its created by other members are retained even if they include the member’s tracks (Article 14(4)).
    3. A post written by the member is retained, with its author shown as “Unknown,” if other members’ tracks or Its remain on it; in this case, the reference video uploaded by the member is detached from the post and handled in the same manner as subparagraph 1. A post with no content from other members is handled in the same manner as subparagraph 1.
  5. Its that have already been shared or saved outside the Service (such as on social media) are beyond the Operator’s control, and the Operator therefore cannot guarantee their retrieval or deletion.
  6. For 30 days after deleting your account, you cannot sign up again with the same social account. If you delete your account while suspended under Article 19, you cannot sign up again until that suspension period ends (up to 1 year). The information the Operator retains for this purpose, and its retention period, are governed by Article 3 of the JAMIT Privacy Policy.

  1. Tracks may include a member’s face, body, and voice, and their musical performance.
  2. By uploading a track to a post and joining a jam, a member is deemed to consent to the following:
    1. The track being shown and played to other users within the Service
    2. The member’s track (including the member’s likeness, voice, and performance) being included in the combination when another member who joined the same post creates an Its

  3. The creator of an Its must include at least one track they uploaded themselves; an Its cannot be created from other people’s tracks alone.
  4. A member may withdraw consent to the future creation of Its by deleting their track. However, the withdrawal may not extend to Its that were already created and saved to a device, or shared outside the Service, before the deletion. Even if a member deletes their account, the member’s tracks included in Its created by other members are retained together with those Its, and the member consents to this by joining a jam. Its created by the member themselves are handled in accordance with Article 7(4), subparagraph 2.

However, in rankings by part (instrument), ranks are shown regardless of any blocking between users.
